* **Project Name/Title :** OceanSearch  
* **Summary:** OceanSearch is a highly interactive, scrollytelling web application that takes users on a visual descent through the ocean's depth zones. It blends a breathtaking 3D WebGL underwater ecosystem with real-time UI overlays that simulate AI-driven marine tracking, environmental IoT telemetry, and marine life discovery to create a next-generation educational platform.   
* **Problem being solved :** Traditional environmental data (such as coral bleaching, microplastic accumulation, and overfishing) is often presented in static, dry reports that fail to emotionally engage the general public. This project bridges that gap by visualizing ocean health crises and marine biology in a highly immersive, game-like digital experience, fostering empathy, deep education, and conservation action.   
* **USP (Unique Selling Point)**  
1. Gamified Environmental Empathy (Data visualization via immersive gameplay)  
2. 3D Canvas & DOM UI Integration (Screen-space UI locked to 3D objects)  
3. Dual-Persona Interface (Public narrative portal \+ Admin scientific dashboard)  
4. Procedural Living Ecosystems (Boids AI swarms & dynamic custom shaders)   
* **Key Features :**   
1. Interactive 3D Depth Scrollytelling (Procedural WebGL ocean descent)  
2. AI Benthic Vision Scanner (Real-time 3D marine life identification)  
3. Deep-Sea Drone Fleet Dashboard (AUV telemetry & live camera feeds)  
4. Predictive AI Risk Map (Time-lapse pollution & threat simulation)  
5. Live Incident Logging (Real-time localized threat alerts)  
6. IoT Telemetry Overlay (Depth, temp, pH, and coral stress tracking)  
7. Policy & Environment Simulation (Sandbox for ecological & economic ROI)  
8. Ecosystem "Healing" Mechanics (Interactive 3D environment restoration)
